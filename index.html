About AARAV ESTATES

Building More Than Properties. Building Trust.

AARAV ESTATES is a Sangli-based real estate developer and property consultancy. We help families, professionals and investors find the right property through patient guidance, transparent communication and service that puts the customer first.

Our team listens before we recommend. We take time to understand how you live and work, shortlist options that genuinely fit, walk you through every site and stay beside you through paperwork and possession.

Our Mission

To make property buying simple, honest and rewarding by matching every customer with a space that suits their life and budget.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in real estate across South-West Maharashtra, known for the relationships we build as much as the properties we offer.
